# 01 – Purpose and Scope

## Why a separate repository from AIC-For-NPO?

Non-governmental organizations often face sharper pressures than many domestic non-profits:

- Cross-border funding relationships
- Advocacy and public positioning
- Operating in politically contested spaces
- Higher visibility and reputational stakes

These conditions increase the risk that tools or associations intended to support independence could be pulled into capture dynamics.  

AIC-For-NGO therefore adopts a narrower and more cautious scope than AIC-For-NPO.

## Purpose

To explore, in a limited and transparent way, whether certain AIC principles can help some NGOs:

- Maintain clearer internal decision integrity
- Resist gradual mission drift caused by funding or political pressure
- Keep human judgment central on questions of purpose and ethics
- Document significant decisions in ways that support long-term accountability

## Explicit non-goals

- Becoming a partner in advocacy campaigns
- Providing political or legal strategy advice
- Acting as a funding intermediary or legitimacy provider
- Helping organizations scale visibility or influence
- Diluting AIC’s Third Path or Zero-donation orientation

## Scope boundary

This repository is currently limited to documentation, reflective frameworks, and lightweight templates.  
It does not offer software services or operational support.
