# 05 – Relation to Parallel TestNet

AIC-For-NGO is downstream of, and subordinate to, the development of Parallel TestNet.

## Current reality

Parallel TestNet Phase 0 (Ethical Kernel + basic governance) remains in design and early engineering.  
Therefore this repository contains no runnable claims.

## Future intention

If and when Ethical Kernel and governance evaluation components become testable:

- Decision patterns described here may be translated into scenarios for testing refusal, auditability, and human-gating.
- Feedback from careful real-world reflection can help stress-test whether the kernel mechanisms remain meaningful under NGO-like pressures.

## Design constraint

No pattern or future prototype developed under AIC-For-NGO may weaken or bypass the Ethical-from-kernel direction of the core project.
