## Current major sources of support and influence
List the most significant funding, partnership, and political relationships.

## Influence assessment
For each major relationship:
- What decisions does it currently affect?
- Has its influence grown or shifted in the last period?
- What would be the practical cost of reducing dependence?

## Mission drift signals
Have priorities, language, or willingness to speak on certain issues changed in ways that track external incentives more than internal purpose?

## Structural protections
What existing practices help the organization maintain the ability to refuse?

## Adjustments to consider
Any concrete changes to funding mix, decision processes, or public positioning discipline?

## Overall judgment
Is the organization’s practical independence stronger, stable, or weaker than in the previous period?
