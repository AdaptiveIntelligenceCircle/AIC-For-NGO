# AIC-For-NGO

**Careful adaptation of Adaptive Intelligence Circle principles for Non-Governmental Organizations.**

> This is not a product.  
> This is not an endorsement of any political position.  
> This is not a ready-made toolkit for campaigns or advocacy.  
>  
> This repository explores how some of AIC’s core ideas (Ethical-from-kernel, Third Path, non-capture, Human Meaning) might be relevant to NGOs that value independence and long-term integrity.  
> It remains secondary to the development of Parallel TestNet.

**Status:** Early design / documentation only  
**Parent project:** [Adaptive Intelligence Circle](https://github.com/AdaptiveIntelligenceCircle)  
**Related:** AIC-For-NPO (developed first, lower risk profile)

---

## Purpose

Many NGOs operate in complex environments involving advocacy, international funding, multi-stakeholder coordination, and political sensitivity.  

AIC-For-NGO exists to ask a limited question:

> Can the principles of independence, transparent decision-making, refusal of capture, and protection of human judgment be usefully adapted for some NGOs — without turning AIC into a political or service actor?

The answer is treated as an open hypothesis, not a claim.

---

## Important differences from AIC-For-NPO

- Higher inherent risk of political or funding capture.
- Stronger need for explicit neutrality regarding advocacy positions.
- Greater caution around public association and joint activities.
- Clearer separation between infrastructure thinking and campaign work.

Because of these differences, this repository adopts a more restrictive and under-claim posture than AIC-For-NGO.

---

## Core orientation

| Principle              | Application stance for NGOs                              |
|------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Ethical-from-kernel    | Critical decisions should have visible evaluation points |
| Third Path             | Actively resist both state and large-funder capture      |
| Non-capture            | Funding and partnerships must not quietly rewrite mission |
| Human Meaning          | Protect space for human judgment on purpose and ethics   |
| Transparency           | Significant decisions should be explainable              |
| Under-claim            | Prefer narrow, honest descriptions over ambitious ones   |

---

## What this repository will not do

- Support or oppose specific political causes
- Help NGOs optimize fundraising or visibility strategies
- Provide operational services or fiscal hosting
- Create dependency on AIC
- Serve as a legitimacy brand for organizations that do not practice the underlying disciplines

---

## Relationship to Parallel TestNet

All concrete tools or patterns developed here must remain compatible with the Ethical Kernel direction and should eventually be testable on Parallel TestNet.  
Until Phase 0 of Parallel TestNet is more mature, this repository stays at the level of documentation and careful design.
